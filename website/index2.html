What is Recollia

The product that
remembers,
so you can understand.

Medicine is not about memorising facts. It is about having the right fact available at the right moment — so that understanding, judgement, and action can follow. Recollia holds the facts so your mind is free to do the work that only a doctor can do.

Built for the Indian medical register. Calibrated to the senior clinician's voice. Designed for long study sessions where ivory on dark is not a style choice — it is a decision about your eyes at 11 p.m.

How it works

Three ways to go deeper

Quick Recall

Targeted spaced-repetition cards built from your weak zones. A five-minute round before ward rounds. The facts you need — not the ones you already know.

Active recall · Spaced repetition

Deep Understanding

Socratic dialogue with a tutor calibrated to the senior consultant's register. Ask why a treatment works. Argue a diagnosis. Be challenged without being condescended to.

Concept depth · Clinical reasoning

Daily Plan

A structured study day that accounts for your exam date, your current gaps, and your available hours. Adapts when yesterday's plan slipped. Never guilts you — adjusts instead.

Adaptive scheduling · Exam-aware
